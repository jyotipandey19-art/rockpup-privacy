RockPup Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

RockPup is a mobile application that uses AI-assisted image analysis to help identify rocks from photographs.

Information We Process

Rock photographs: When you use RockPup's identification feature, the photograph you provide is processed to perform rock identification using AI-assisted image analysis.

Device identifier: RockPup generates a random device identifier for the app installation. A one-way cryptographic hash of this identifier is sent to our server and stored. We use this identifier to manage scan limits and subscription entitlements without requiring you to create an account.

Subscription information: If you purchase a RockPup subscription, subscription and entitlement information is processed through the relevant app store and our subscription-management provider. This information is used to determine whether premium features are available to you.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Identify rocks from photographs.
  • Enforce free scan limits.
  • Provide and manage RockPup subscriptions.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the service.
  • Protect the service against misuse and abuse.

Third-Party Service Providers

RockPup uses third-party service providers to operate its features. These may include cloud infrastructure providers, AI-assisted image analysis providers, and subscription-management services.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide the services used by RockPup, subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

RockPup is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by RockPup. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of information. You can contact us using the address below for privacy requests.
